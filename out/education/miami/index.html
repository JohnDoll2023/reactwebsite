    Miami University

    Fall 2019 - Spring 2023

    With high school graduation approaching, I started to tell my parents I wasn't sure that my college experience would be able to top high school. I loved the relationships I had and classes I took throughout my four years at Wapak. I dipped my toes into everything I could, including taking the Intro to Engineering course, which was my only academic exposure to engineering prior to college. I entered Miami as a triple major in Computer Science, Computer Engineering, and Mathematics. I switched majors not one, not two, not three, not four, not five, but six times, ultimately landing solely on computer science. Having an innate curiousness for all of engineering, I wanted to try a lot of different things, but never swayed from computer science. My junior and senior years, I focused heavily on taking electives within the computer science department for all the special courses they offered. All in all, including my transfer credits from high school, I graduated with 190 credit hours.

    Involvements

    Undergraduate Associate (UA)

    Fall 2020

    In the Fall of 2020, I volunteered and was lucky enough to be selected as an Undergraduate Associate for a section of Computing, Engineering, and Society (CEC 101). Two other UA's (who happened to be my friends) and I were responsible for most things outside the classroom except for grading. We would host weekly office hours on Zoom as all classes at Miami were virtual for that semester. We would have a few kids show up, and we actually became friends with a couple of them which was awesome; I'm still friends with them today. It was nice to be on the other side of the things as the "instructor" for once just to gain a new perspective. It has helped me to better understand and connect with my professors as I now know the struggles they face and as a student have more to offer to the professor having taken a position similar to theirs. Other responsibilities we had as UA's was creating and distributing weekly newsletters with topics ranging from the class specifically to university-wide events. Additionally, we were "buddies" with the first-year students as they got accustomed to college life during the COVID era. I feel like we were able to have a stronger impact this particular year as opposed to others because the students were having an extremely tough time getting connected to others and finding resources that on-campus students would have no trouble doing. This was one of the most rewarding parts of the position as I personally was able to help students find their way through their first semester at Miami.

    Honors Ambassador

    Panelist (Fall 2020 - Spring 2023)
    Breakfast Greeter (Fall 2021 - Spring 2023)
    Presidential Showcase Ambassador (Spring 2021 - Spring 2023)
    Impromptu Guide on many occasions (Fall 2020 - Spring 2023)

    I was recommended for the Honors Ambassador position by several faculty members after I attended a recruiting event with my sister, who was a senior in high school at the time. I, a student in just my first semester, was with her along with my parents to more or less hang out and get to see each other again. For my last panel, I wore a special Mickey Mouse hat for fun and it ended up being the day that the university photographer was there.     Senior Design Project

    My year-long senior design project was to build a machine learning model and system that would automatically detect and recogize license plates entering and exiting a parking garage. The university sponsored this project as they are looking to find a much cheaper alternative for parking garage access than the current system. The current system is through an annual contract costing over $1,000,000 which is on top of each of the $10,000 cameras that are installed in each garage and on several golf carts they drive around to check for parking passes. Our budget was $150 and with this, we were able to create the system with one camera that was attached to a Raspberry Pi. The system was able to detect and recognize license plates in real time and then send the data to a database. The system was also able to detect and read the characters on the license plate. We had users set up that could sign up with their plate and see their entry and exit log. We also had admins that could see and correct all of the data and add/remove users. I am real proud of my team and our success on the project. At the senior design expo, we placed 2nd out of over 30 teams.
